GSTT Work Experience – Important Information

Eligibility – Students Attending School or College in the Four Boroughs

If you attend a school or college in Southwark, Lambeth, Hillingdon, or the Royal Borough of Kensington & Chelsea:

  • Your school must contact us first to confirm their support for your application.
  • We cannot accept direct requests from students or parents without prior contact from the school or college.
  • Once your school has confirmed their support, you may complete the Microsoft Form provided.
  • We aim to offer a maximum of two placements per school each year, subject to availability.

Eligibility – Residents of the Four Boroughs

If you live in Southwark, Lambeth, Hillingdon, or the Royal Borough of Kensington & Chelsea but attend a school or college outside these areas:

  • You may still be eligible for a placement.
  • Please contact us directly to discuss your circumstances before applying.
  • All placements are subject to availability and may be prioritised for students attending schools within the four boroughs.

If You Are Not Eligible

If you neither attend a school or college in, nor live in, one of the four boroughs listed above, we are unable to offer you a placement. You should contact your local healthcare Trust to explore opportunities closer to your area.

Careers Events – Schools and Colleges Only

  • We support careers events for schools and colleges within the four boroughs listed above.
  • Please provide at least six weeks’ notice for careers event requests.
  • Support is dependent on staff availability.
  • Once your school has contacted us, you may then complete the Microsoft Form.
