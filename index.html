Widening Participation

Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust is committed to making NHS careers accessible to all, particularly for individuals from underrepresented or disadvantaged backgrounds. Recognising the barriers that can limit access to healthcare professions, the Trust actively works with local schools, colleges, and community organisations to offer meaningful opportunities. Through work experience placements, apprenticeships, internships, and targeted training programmes, participants gain practical skills, knowledge, and confidence to pursue careers in healthcare. These initiatives not only enhance employability but also inspire and empower residents by highlighting the wide range of NHS roles. Guided by our ambitious, inclusive, and caring values, we create accessible pathways that remove barriers, nurture potential, and help build a more representative, compassionate, and socially mobile healthcare workforce for the future.
