📌 Important – Information to Read Before Applying for Work Experience

Placement Types
Clinical placements are strictly observational. Students will not be permitted to carry out any form of hands-on patient care.
Non-clinical placements may include roles such as administrative or office-based work.

Restricted Areas
No placements in operating theatres, maternity units, ICU, or other critical care areas.

Supervision and Data Access
Students will be supervised at all times. No access to patient records or confidential data.

Placement Duration
Standard: 1–2 weeks. Extended: up to 4 weeks if agreed in advance.

Induction Requirements
Managers must provide induction covering health & safety, dress code, and confidentiality.

ID Badges
Not usually required. If needed, managers can request one.

DBS Checks
Not usually required. If needed (16+), student/school must cover cost.

Clinical Placement Protocol
Students must be identified and introduced as students. Verbal patient consent required.

IT Access
Supervisor must request login details in advance.

Post-Placement Feedback
Students will be asked to complete a feedback form.
