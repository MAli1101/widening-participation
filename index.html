Widening Participation

Guy’s and St Thomas NHS Foundation Trust aims to make NHS careers accessible to everyone, particularly people from underrepresented or disadvantaged backgrounds. We work closely with local schools, colleges, and community organisations to offer work experience, apprenticeships, and training opportunities, helping local residents gain skills, confidence, and a pathway into healthcare careers.
