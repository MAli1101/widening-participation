GSTT Work Experience – Guidelines & Information

1. Clinical Placement Restrictions

  • Students are not permitted to undertake any hands-on patient care.
  • They may carry out minor, risk-assessed tasks under supervision (e.g. simple admin tasks, helping to carry light equipment).
  • Students will not have access to patient data and must be supervised at all times.
  • Students will have the opportunity to shadow and observe staff, performing supervised tasks to develop their knowledge and skills.
  • We cannot offer placements in operating theatres, maternity, intensive care, or other critical care areas.

2. Placement Duration

Typical placements last 1–2 weeks (up to 4 weeks if agreed in advance). We aim to accommodate the dates requested by the school/college, subject to departmental availability.

3. Induction Requirements

  • Health & safety (infection prevention, fire safety, evacuation procedures)
  • General orientation (toilets, canteen, break arrangements)
  • Code of conduct, dress code, confidentiality, and data protection

4. Identification & Security

ID badges are not usually required, as students must be accompanied at all times. If beneficial (e.g. observing in patient clinics), departments may request an ID card here.

5. Disclosure & Barring Service (DBS)

DBS checks are not required for work experience students, as they are always supervised. In sensitive areas, managers may request DBS clearance for students aged 16+ (costs borne by student or school/college).

6. Feedback

At the end of the placement, students should complete a feedback form to help us improve the quality and impact of our work experience programme.

7. Additional Guidance for Clinical Observations

  • Supervisors must ensure students are clearly identifiable as students and introduced as such to patients.
  • Verbal consent must be obtained from patients before observation of any consultation, treatment, or procedure.
  • Students must be informed they are not pre-registration healthcare students (e.g. not student nurses, midwives, or doctors).
  • Patient privacy and dignity must be maintained at all times.

8. IT & System Access

If IT access (e.g. login details, email account) is required, the line manager must request this in advance for the placement student.
