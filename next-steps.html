GSTT Work Experience – Guidelines & Information

1. Clinical Placement Restrictions

  • No hands-on patient care is permitted.
  • Students may assist with minor, risk-assessed tasks under supervision (e.g. admin duties, carrying light equipment).
  • Access to patient data is strictly prohibited; supervision is required at all times.
  • Students may shadow and observe staff to build knowledge and skills.
  • Placements are not available in operating theatres, maternity, intensive care, or other critical care areas.

2. Placement Duration

Placements typically last 1–2 weeks, with the option to extend up to 4 weeks if agreed. We aim to match requested dates with departmental availability.

3. Induction Requirements

  • Health & safety: infection prevention, fire safety, evacuation procedures
  • General orientation: toilets, canteen, break arrangements
  • Code of conduct, dress code, confidentiality, and data protection

4. Identification & Security

ID badges are not usually required, as students must be accompanied at all times. If needed (e.g. observing in clinics), departments may request an ID card here.

5. Disclosure & Barring Service (DBS)

DBS checks are not required for work experience students, as they are always supervised. In sensitive areas, managers may request DBS clearance for students aged 16+, with costs covered by the student or school/college.

6. Feedback

Students should complete a feedback form at the end of their placement to help improve the programme.

7. Additional Guidance for Clinical Observations

  • Students must be clearly identified and introduced to patients as students.
  • Verbal consent must be obtained before observing any consultation, treatment, or procedure.
  • Students must be informed they are not pre-registration healthcare professionals.
  • Patient privacy and dignity must be upheld at all times.

8. IT & System Access

If IT access (e.g. login credentials, email accounts) is required, the line manager must request this in advance.
