📌 Important – Information to Read Before Applying for Work Experience

🔍 Placement Types

Clinical placements are strictly observational. You will not be permitted to undertake any hands-on clinical tasks, including taking blood, administering medication, or assisting with procedures. These placements are designed to give you insight into the working environment and roles within the NHS.

🧠 Expectations

  • You must be proactive, punctual, and professionally dressed.
  • You are expected to engage respectfully with staff and patients.
  • Confidentiality must be maintained at all times—no photography or recording is allowed.

📝 Documentation

  • You must complete the Work Experience Application Form in full.
  • Parental/guardian consent is required if you are under 18.
  • Proof of identity and address may be requested before placement begins.

🛡️ Insurance and Liability

All placements are covered under the Trust’s public liability insurance. You will not be financially liable for any incidents occurring during your placement, provided you follow all guidance and instructions.

🧭 Next Steps

  • Complete the application form
  • Await confirmation from the Widening Participation team
  • Prepare for your placement by reviewing the Trust’s values and expectations
